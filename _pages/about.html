Research

I use tools from quantum information theory, homological algebra, and graph theory to build quantum error-correcting codes and fault-tolerant protocols. I also work on quantum algorithms and quantum metrology. My goal is to help build quantum computers that can withstand decoherence and tackle problems beyond the reach of classical computers.
